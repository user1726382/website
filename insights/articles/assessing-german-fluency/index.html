How to assess German fluency in recruitment

Design a fair, job-relevant language assessment for German-facing roles.

Updated: 21 October 2026

← Back to insights

Map language to tasks

A customer-support role may require clear explanations and empathy, while an enterprise sales role may require negotiation and commercial writing. Define observable requirements.

Use consistent exercises

Where appropriate, ask candidates to complete the same realistic task: explain a product issue, summarise a customer email or conduct a discovery conversation.

Assess both languages separately

German customer communication and English internal collaboration are different requirements. Evaluate them against their actual use.

Document relevant evidence

Use a clear rubric covering comprehension, accuracy, tone and task completion. Avoid irrelevant questions or assumptions based on nationality.

Protect candidate data

Only collect information needed for recruitment, explain its use and retention, and apply the same standards consistently.
