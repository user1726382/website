Hiring German-speaking employees in Ireland

An employer's practical guide to defining, attracting and assessing German-speaking talent.

Updated: 21 October 2026

← Back to insights

Define the role before the language

Start with outcomes, daily responsibilities, seniority, team structure and working model. Decide whether German is needed for customer conversations, technical documentation, negotiations or all three. The language requirement should reflect the work.

Make compensation credible

Separate annual gross base salary from commission, OTE, bonuses and benefits. Compare relevant Irish roles and explain how the location and office attendance affect the offer.

Consider candidate experience

Candidates based in Germany may need clarity on relocation, housing, Irish employment terms and interview scheduling. Clear communication can prevent avoidable drop-off.

Agree the hiring process

Set a consistent screening framework, interview ownership, decision criteria and response times before approaching candidates.

Discuss a hiring brief

The Link focuses initially on German-speaking commercial and customer-facing roles in Ireland. Share the role, salary range, location and hiring timeline.
