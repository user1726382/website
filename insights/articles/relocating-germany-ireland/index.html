Relocating from Germany to Ireland for work

A practical checklist for German-speaking professionals preparing for an Irish role.

Updated: 21 October 2026

← Back to insights

Confirm the employment details

Review the written offer, employer identity, start date, location, pay structure, working hours and probation terms before making commitments.

Plan housing early

Compare realistic accommodation options and commuting routes. Read the Residential Tenancies Board guidance on deposits and tenancy rights before paying money.

Prepare payroll arrangements

Check PPSN requirements and register a first Irish job through Revenue myAccount when applicable. Delays may lead to emergency tax.

Arrange essential services

Research healthcare access through the HSE, banking requirements, mobile connectivity and local transport.

Compare total compensation

Consider base salary, commission, pension, relocation assistance and recurring living expenses, rather than converting gross salaries directly.
