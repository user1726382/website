Where to find German-speaking sales professionals

A practical sourcing framework for Irish commercial teams.

Updated: 21 October 2026

← Back to insights

Start with the sales motion

An SDR for outbound prospecting, an SMB account executive and an enterprise seller need different experience. Define territory, buyer profile, sales cycle and quota expectations.

Search across markets

Consider German-speaking professionals already in Ireland as well as candidates based in Germany and other relevant European markets, subject to the role's location and employment requirements.

Assess the right skills

Look beyond fluency. Evaluate discovery calls, qualification, CRM discipline, negotiation and relevant customer experience for the specific level.

Make the offer competitive

Explain base salary, variable pay, realistic targets, hybrid policy and career progression. For relocation, provide accurate practical information.

Keep the process efficient

Agree clear selection criteria, interviewer ownership and feedback expectations. The best sourcing plan still needs a responsive process.
