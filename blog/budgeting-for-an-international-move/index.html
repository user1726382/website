Budgeting for an international move

General information only; not individualized legal, tax, medical or immigration advice. Verify current requirements before acting.

Separate one-off moving costs from recurring expenses. Include deposits, temporary housing, travel, insurance, transport and an emergency buffer. Treat salary calculators as estimates and compare like-for-like household assumptions rather than relying on headline net income.
