Budgeting for an international move

Separate one-off moving costs from recurring expenses. Include deposits, temporary housing, travel, insurance, transport and an emergency buffer. Treat salary calculators as estimates and compare like-for-like household assumptions rather than relying on headline net income.

Education and qualifications across the EU

Recognition of qualifications depends on the profession and destination country. Regulated professions may require formal recognition; employers may assess non-regulated qualifications independently. Check the competent authority before paying for translations or credential assessments.

Healthcare when relocating between Ireland and Germany

Healthcare eligibility depends on residence, employment, insurance status and cross-border coordination rules. Confirm coverage before travelling, especially for family members and ongoing treatment. An EHIC does not automatically replace comprehensive coverage when relocating.

How to assess a German-speaking job offer in Ireland

Review the employer identity, written terms, working location, salary structure, language expectations and recruitment contact. Never pay a recruiter to secure a job offer. Verify opportunities directly with the employer and avoid sending sensitive identity documents through unverified channels.

Practical guide · Published 2026-10-21

Moving from Germany to Ireland: the first-month checklist

Before departure, confirm your job contract, housing budget, identification, and healthcare arrangements. After arrival, review PPS number eligibility and application steps, tax registration, banking, and tenancy documentation. Allow time for accommodation searches and retain digital copies of essential records.

Moving from Ireland to Germany: a practical checklist

Plan your housing, health insurance, employment documentation and local registration. Anmeldung requirements and appointment availability vary by municipality. Check your tax identification details, bank account needs, and insurance start date before relying on a fixed timeline.

Practical guide · Published 2026-10-21

Negotiating salary in Germany

Compare annual gross pay, bonuses, working hours, collective agreements where applicable, paid leave and commuting costs. Ask how the bonus is measured and when salary reviews occur. Avoid comparing net pay across countries without accounting for individual circumstances.

Practical guide · Published 2026-10-21

Negotiating salary in Ireland

Compare base pay separately from variable compensation, pension contributions, health benefits and relocation support. Ask about on-target earnings assumptions, probation, notice periods and whether salary figures are gross annual amounts. Negotiate based on documented role scope and market evidence.
