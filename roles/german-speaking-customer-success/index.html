About the Role

Customer success managers help business customers adopt a product, reach agreed outcomes and maintain a productive relationship over time. German-speaking CSMs may support organisations in Germany, Austria and Switzerland from an Irish office or hybrid team. The role often starts with understanding the customer's intended results, then building an adoption plan, coordinating training and identifying risks that could undermine value. German-language ability supports effective stakeholder conversations and nuanced explanations; English helps align with international product, support and commercial colleagues. Depending on the employer, customer success may be primarily advisory or may include renewals and expansion responsibilities. Successful CSMs combine empathy with structured account management, product knowledge and the ability to interpret usage or health indicators. Career paths can lead toward strategic customer success, implementation consulting, customer operations or leadership. Account ownership, portfolio size, on-call expectations and performance incentives are employer-specific and should not be assumed from a general career profile.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead onboarding and success-planning discussions with German-speaking customers.
  • Agree measurable customer outcomes and review progress regularly.
  • Support product adoption through training, enablement and stakeholder engagement.
  • Monitor usage and account-health signals to identify potential risks.
  • Coordinate complex customer issues with support and product specialists.
  • Document customer objectives, commitments and interactions in the CRM.
  • Prepare business reviews that connect product use to customer priorities.
  • Collaborate on renewal or expansion planning where the role requires it.

Essential Requirements

  • Professional German fluency and good English communication.
  • Experience in customer success, account management, implementation or consulting.
  • Ability to build trusted relationships with varied customer stakeholders.
  • Structured problem-solving and strong presentation skills.
  • Confidence learning products and interpreting customer engagement data.

Desirable Qualifications

  • Experience with SaaS onboarding or customer-health platforms.
  • Familiarity with Gainsight, Salesforce or similar systems.

What You Can Expect

Depending on the employer, opportunities may include:

  • Competitive base salary depending on employer and experience
  • Potential performance-related bonus or commission where relevant
  • International working environment and cross-border collaboration
  • Training, professional development and possible career progression
  • Possible hybrid working or relocation support where offered

These are possible arrangements, not confirmed benefits or offers.

About the Company

Customer success teams in Ireland may serve DACH customers for international software, cloud and digital-service providers. The employer's product, customer portfolio and commercial ownership will be specified when a vacancy is authorised.
