# 2026 Irish salary benchmarking — internal methodology

**Date:** 20 October 2026. **Location:** Dublin / Ireland. **Currency:** EUR annual gross base salary (excluding variable OTE, equity and benefits).

These are **modelled recruiting salary ranges**, not confirmed salary budgets or offers from named employers. The site should not describe a position as an active employer-authorised vacancy until the actual employer, requisition and hiring terms have been verified. The words “Apply Now” refer to an email enquiry/application to The Link; they do not establish that a named employer has an active opening.

## Calculation

For each lower and upper endpoint:

`published Irish base-salary benchmark × (1 + assumed German-language / DACH expertise premium) × 1.10`, rounded to the nearest €1,000.

The **additional 10%** is the explicit commercial uplift requested by the website owners. The **German-language premium** is a modelling assumption, **not a published universal market statistic**. It is set at 0% where the underlying benchmark is already multilingual or the DACH requirement is integral to the executive title, 3–4% for highly specialised senior/technical roles, and 5% for other roles where German-market fluency may increase competition. **Do not claim the premium has been empirically measured.** Premiums can be zero or vary substantially by employer.

Some base bands use close title proxies where no exact title-and-German-specific public benchmark is available; particularly senior executives, partnerships, solutions consulting, marketing leadership and customer success. These are judgemental estimates informed by the cited guides, not exact extracted median observations.

## Governance before employer-specific posting

Verify an employer's actual authorisation, location, salary budget, employment terms and privacy/legal basis before advertising a specific live job. Do not use `JobPosting` schema for general career categories. Confirm the business operator's legal identity and complete recruitment-specific privacy notices before collecting CVs. The static site does not provide CV uploads; candidates attach files themselves to email.
