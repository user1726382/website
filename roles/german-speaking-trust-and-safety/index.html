About the Role

Trust and safety specialists help online services apply their rules consistently and protect users from harmful, fraudulent or abusive activity. A German-speaking specialist may review user reports, account behaviour or policy questions relating to German-language content while working within an international operations team in Ireland. Strong German comprehension is important for understanding context, idioms and nuance; English is typically needed for internal policy guidance, escalations and quality reviews. The work can involve structured decision-making, accurate case documentation and collaboration with specialist teams handling complex or urgent issues. Some trust and safety roles involve exposure to upsetting material or shift schedules, so candidates should receive a clear, specific explanation of working conditions before accepting an offer. Employers may provide training, wellness resources and defined escalation procedures, but these should not be assumed. Experience can lead to quality assurance, policy operations, investigations, risk operations or team leadership. The content categories, safety measures and schedule vary by employer.

Key Responsibilities

  • Review German-language reports or cases against documented platform policies.
  • Assess context and evidence before taking or recommending action.
  • Record decisions and rationales accurately in internal case systems.
  • Escalate urgent, ambiguous or high-risk cases through designated channels.
  • Identify recurring abuse patterns and share observations with specialists.
  • Follow confidentiality, privacy and information-security procedures.
  • Participate in quality reviews and policy-update training.
  • Apply consistent standards while meeting appropriate accuracy goals.

Essential Requirements

  • Professional German comprehension and good written English.
  • Careful judgement and ability to follow detailed policies.
  • Strong attention to context, consistency and accurate documentation.
  • Resilience and willingness to discuss role-specific content exposure.
  • Ability to protect sensitive information and escalate uncertainty.

Desirable Qualifications

  • Experience in content operations, moderation, customer support or investigations.
  • Familiarity with online-platform policies and quality-review processes.

What You Can Expect

Depending on the employer, opportunities may include:

  • Competitive base salary depending on employer and experience
  • Potential performance-related bonus or commission where relevant
  • International working environment and cross-border collaboration
  • Training, professional development and possible career progression
  • Possible hybrid working or relocation support where offered

Benefits and arrangements vary by employer.

About the Company

Potential employers include online marketplaces, social platforms and outsourced trust-and-safety operations serving European users. Any exposure to sensitive content, shift arrangements and employee support measures must be disclosed by the confirmed employer.
