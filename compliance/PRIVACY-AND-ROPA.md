# GDPR governance and record of processing — working policy
Version 0.1, 20 October 2026. Controller and privacy lead: [TO CONFIRM].

## Policy
Apply GDPR Arts. 5, 6, 9, 10, 12–22, 24–25, 28, 30, 32–36 and 37–39 as relevant. Document lawful bases per purpose; do not treat a privacy acknowledgement as consent. Minimize data, limit access, secure transfers, and set proportionate retention. Assess German §38 BDSG DPO criteria separately.

## Preliminary processing inventory
| Activity | Data | Potential legal basis (verify) | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static hosting and security logs | IP and technical data | Legitimate interests Art. 6(1)(f) | PREVIEW |
| Business enquiries | Work contact and message | Art. 6(1)(f), or 6(1)(b) where applicable | DISABLED |
| Client services and invoices | Contacts, contracts, payment data | Art. 6(1)(b)/(c)/(f) by context | NOT LIVE |
| Direct candidate applications | CV, employment history, correspondence | Determine recruitment basis and local employment law | DISABLED |
| Sourced candidates | Professional profile, source, notes | Document Art. 6(1)(f) balancing and Art. 14 notice | DISABLED |
| Talent pool | CV, role preferences | Separate lawful basis and notice | DISABLED |
| Marketing lists | Contact, source, preference and suppression | GDPR and ePrivacy/UWG assessment | NOT LIVE |
| Staff/admin security | Account identifiers and access events | Legitimate interests/security duty as applicable | NOT DEPLOYED |

Complete for each processing activity: actual controller, purpose, categories, recipients, transfers, vendors, erasure schedule, security measures, lawful basis, source, Art. 9/10 conditions if any, and responsible owner.

## DPIA screening
Screen before high-risk processing for evaluation/scoring, solely automated significant decisions, large-scale or sensitive data, vulnerable people, monitoring, novel technology, data matching and relevant national supervisory authority DPIA lists. Document why Art. 35 does or does not apply. If required, perform and approve a DPIA *before* processing. Candidate AI scoring is not approved.

## Rights
Record request date, verify identity proportionately, locate records, apply lawful exceptions and normally respond within one month; explain lawful extensions. Inform processors/recipients where required. Maintain minimal audit evidence.

## Transfers and vendors
Inventory processors and sign Art. 28 DPAs. Review international transfers outside the EEA and any SCCs/adequacy decisions. Do not assume a controller-to-controller disclosure is an Art. 28 processor relationship.

## Governance
Assign a privacy owner, review register and supplier list at least annually and on material changes, record incidents and approve policies before processing.
