# Direct marketing, AI and website tracking policy
DRAFT — requires campaign-by-campaign approval.

## Outbound marketing
Record campaign owner, audience, source, jurisdiction, GDPR basis and ePrivacy/UWG permission before contact. Germany: promotional email generally requires prior consent, including B2B, subject to narrow statutory exceptions; B2B calls have separate strict rules. Ireland: apply ePrivacy rules and recipient distinctions. Do not treat a public email address or bought list as permission. Provide sender identity, required notices and functioning opt-out. Maintain minimal suppression records and honor objections.

## Client marketing
Determine whether The Link is controller, processor or joint controller for each campaign. Execute Art. 28 DPA when processing on documented instructions; record list provenance, approvals, retention, vendors, transfers and data rights ownership. No retargeting or analytics cookies before required consent and documentation.

## AI
Keep an AI tool register with provider, purpose, data types, processing role, location, training/retention terms and owner. No uploading CVs, client secrets or sensitive information into unapproved consumer AI tools. No automated candidate ranking, selection or rejection until GDPR Arts. 22/35 and EU AI Act high-risk assessment, human oversight, transparency, bias testing and vendor contracts are complete. Follow current phased AI Act deadlines rather than assuming every provision applies immediately.

## Content and advertising
Verify factual claims, customer permission for testimonials, copyright/image rights, advertisement labeling, comparative claims and local consumer/B2B rules. Do not invent credentials, licensing, placements or case studies.
