## Establishment and entity
- [ ] Confirm German sole trader, UG/GmbH, Irish LTD or other operator; where management and permanent establishments are located.
- [ ] Check name/trademark availability and ownership of the proposed domain.
- [ ] Complete German Gewerbeanmeldung / Handelsregister if applicable and Finanzamt registration.
- [ ] Assess Irish CRO business-name/company registration and Irish permanent establishment obligations if operating there.
- [ ] Confirm Irish employment agency licensing with WRC before Irish recruitment operations; confirm German AÜG/AVGS relevance separately.
- [ ] Identify managing representatives, registered office and serviceable address for DDG §5 and Irish company website disclosures.

## Tax and invoicing
- [ ] Obtain correct tax identifiers and VAT registrations; assess German Kleinunternehmer rules if relevant and Irish VAT registration triggers.
- [ ] Determine place of supply and reverse-charge treatment for each DE/IE cross-border B2B service, with exceptions.
- [ ] Validate customer VAT numbers where needed and issue invoices with correct legal and VAT details.
- [ ] Prepare to receive German structured e-invoices; assess phased obligations to issue them.
- [ ] Set retention and secure accounting archive according to applicable national law.
- [ ] Assess Irish payroll, employer registration, A1/social security and employment law if hiring workers there.
- [ ] Assess permanent establishment and transfer-pricing exposure if related entities or substantial Irish presence develop.

## Insurance and commercial operations
- [ ] Review professional indemnity (professional errors), public liability, cyber insurance and employers' liability if staff hired.
- [ ] Establish dedicated business account, accounting system, authorized signatories and anti-fraud payment verification.
- [ ] Implement supplier due diligence, conflicts of interest, sanctions screening where applicable and confidentiality controls.
- [ ] Confirm copyright licences, advertising substantiation, testimonials and marketing claims.
