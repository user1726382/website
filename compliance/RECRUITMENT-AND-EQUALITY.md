# Recruitment, candidate handling and equal opportunity policy
DRAFT — requires German and Irish legal approval before recruitment starts.

## Authorization gate
Permanent placement, candidate introductions, recruitment advertising, temporary worker supply and immigration/legal advice are distinct services. Verify Irish WRC employment agency licence requirements for actual territorial operations. Do not operate a licensable Irish agency without authorization. Assess German AÜG authorization before any employee leasing; assess AZAV for AVGS reimbursement. Do not charge jobseekers prohibited placement fees.

## Fair recruitment
Define objective role-related criteria before advertising. Apply German AGG and Irish Employment Equality Acts. Advertisements and interviews must avoid discriminatory criteria and unjustified sensitive questions. Provide reasonable accommodation and accessible channels where applicable. Do not publish fictitious jobs or clients.

## Candidate sourcing and transparency
Record lawful source, collection date, specific vacancy, processing purpose and lawful basis. Provide Art. 13 notice at collection; provide Art. 14 notice for indirect sourcing within applicable timelines and exceptions. Avoid indiscriminate scraping and use of personal contact data for marketing.

## Selection and introductions
Minimize CV fields. No routine health, criminal conviction, religion or family-status collection. Document human-led assessment and review fairness. No automated candidate scoring or solely automated significant decisions without dedicated legal review. Identify roles of agency and client as independent controllers, joint controllers or controller/processor according to reality. Notify candidates about recipients and disclose only necessary information securely.

## References, checks and retention
Obtain legally required permissions for references/background checks; restrict to relevant information. Store candidate files in access-controlled systems. Apply approved retention by vacancy and country; treat talent pools as separate processing with a separate basis and notice. Implement candidate access, objection, correction and erasure procedures.

## Candidate complaints
Designate [COMPLAINTS CONTACT]. Log allegations of bias or misuse, preserve necessary evidence, protect confidentiality and escalate to the privacy lead. Annual policy review and staff training.
