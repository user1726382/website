# Accessibility, content, digital regulation and publishing policy
DRAFT — scope decisions and accessibility testing pending.

## Accessibility
Assess EU Accessibility Act and German BFSG applicability to actual consumer-facing service, including any e-commerce function and microenterprise exemption; do not assume all B2B informational sites are covered. Regardless, target WCAG 2.2 AA good practice. Test keyboard-only navigation, focus order and visibility, headings/landmarks, color contrast, 200%/400% zoom, screen readers, form labels/errors, language attributes and mobile. Record actual defects and remediation; never claim certification without independent evidence.

## Public claims
Every service, client case study, qualification, recruitment vacancy, partner, license, guarantee and testimonial must be accurate and supported by approval/evidence. Do not publish fake clients, placements, badges, regulator permissions or guaranteed outcomes. Confirm rights to images, fonts, brand marks and copy. Identify advertising and affiliate content when required.

## Regulatory scope screening
- Digital Services Act: assess if future user-generated content, hosting or platform services are added; ordinary corporate brochure website not automatically an intermediary platform.
- NIS2 and German implementation: screen sector, size and special inclusion; document decision.
- AI Act: maintain AI inventory and employment screening classification; phase-in deadlines change and must be checked before use.
- MStV: check responsible editor disclosure if journalistic-editorial content is published in Germany.
- Consumer law: do not introduce consumer checkout without reviewing precontract information, withdrawal, pricing and accessibility requirements.
- Marketing: comply with GDPR, ePrivacy and Germany's UWG §7 as applicable.

## Release gate
Content owner approves all claims; privacy owner approves notices and tracking; technical owner tests accessibility and security; legal reviewer signs off operator disclosures, regulated activities and terms. Keep preview noindex until approvals recorded.
