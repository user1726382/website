# Candidate data sharing protocol — DRAFT
Use when recruiter and hiring employer are independent GDPR controllers. If they jointly determine purposes/means, assess Art. 26; if one acts only on documented instructions, use Art. 28.

Recruiter [ENTITY/PRIVACY CONTACT]; Hiring employer [ENTITY/PRIVACY CONTACT]; specific vacancy [ ]; countries [ ]; legal bases and candidate notices [ ].

1. Recruiter shares only role-relevant candidate information after lawful sourcing, transparency and a documented disclosure basis.
2. Employer uses received information only for stated recruitment purpose unless a separate lawful basis and notice apply; no unrelated marketing, resale or onward sharing.
3. Both parties implement least-privilege access, secure transmission, appropriate retention and incident handling.
4. Each controller responds to rights requests and supervisory inquiries concerning its own processing and cooperates where necessary.
5. Each controller provides applicable Articles 13/14 notices, including recipients and transfers; neither relies on the other's notice without verification.
6. Special-category and criminal-offence information is excluded unless separately lawful and necessary.
7. Each controller applies a justified erasure schedule and coordinates correction of inaccurate candidate records.
8. International transfers outside the EEA require a separate GDPR Chapter V assessment.
