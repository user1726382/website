Your practical Ireland guide

Finding German-speaking jobs

Compare responsibilities, employer requirements, location and working arrangements. A general career profile is not automatically an active vacancy.

Salary expectations

Compare annual gross base pay, pension, benefits, bonus and commission. On-target earnings (OTE) are not guaranteed income.

Dublin and other Irish locations

Consider job availability, housing, commuting and local services. The right location depends on the role and personal circumstances.

Cost of living

Budget for accommodation, deposit, utilities, transport and initial expenses. Verify prices with current listings.

Renting and accommodation

Understand the lease, deposit, landlord details and tenant rights. The Residential Tenancies Board provides authoritative guidance.

Employment contracts and probation

Review the written terms, probation, working hours, leave, notice and benefits. Check current Irish rules with the Workplace Relations Commission.

PPS number

Check how to apply for a Personal Public Service Number (PPSN) and which documents are required.

Tax and payroll

Register your first Irish job through Revenue myAccount promptly where applicable, to avoid emergency tax.

Healthcare and insurance

Review public healthcare access, GP registration and any private health benefits. Refer to HSE information.

Banking and everyday services

Check identification and address requirements for banking or payment services; plan utilities, mobile and transport.

Relocation preparation

Confirm employment eligibility, signed offer, accommodation, travel, documents and the practical first week.

Interviews and CVs

Prepare a concise, role-focused CV with achievements; practise English interviews and German-language role scenarios.

Sales compensation

Ask about base pay, OTE, quota, ramp-up, territory and commission triggers before comparing offers.

Hybrid and remote work

Verify office attendance and location restrictions. Cross-border working may have tax and social-security implications.

Annual leave and working conditions

Review leave entitlements and public holiday treatment using current WRC information, rather than assuming German rules apply.

Settling into Ireland

Plan practical arrival steps, local transport, healthcare access and professional networks.

Choosing a role

Consider the actual tasks, progression, team culture and long-term fit, not only the German-language premium.

Next steps

Explore role categories and contact us to discuss your interests; specific employer vacancies must be verified.

Official resources

Rules, procedures and costs change. Check current details with the relevant official sources.

Candidate questions

Can I apply from Germany?

You can explore Irish career paths while living in Germany. Check eligibility, location and working arrangements for any confirmed vacancy.

What German and English levels are required?

Requirements vary by employer and function. Strong German and practical English are common in international customer-facing roles.

Are jobs remote or hybrid?

This depends on the employer and must be confirmed for each actual vacancy.

How do salaries and OTE work?

Our salary guide shows modelled annual gross base pay. OTE and commission depend on the employer's plan.

Do employers help with relocation?

Relocation support varies. Ask the employer about travel, accommodation and settling-in support.

What happens after I enquire?

We can discuss your interests and clarify whether a specific opportunity is available. General career profiles are not confirmed vacancies.

How is my data handled?

Review the candidate privacy information before sharing personal details. There is no CV upload form or automated candidate ranking.
