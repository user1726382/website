Retrospective coverage of major EU and German–Irish developments

Historical topics below are newly written retrospective summaries, not articles originally published in those years. Published 21 October 2026. Rules and guidance may change; verify current requirements with the linked authorities.
Retrospective / Analysis · Published 2026-10-21

2018: GDPR reshapes European business data practices

Retrospective: The EU General Data Protection Regulation began applying in May 2018. It remains central to recruitment, candidate privacy and international business operations. Organizations should verify their specific controller or processor responsibilities.

Retrospective / Analysis · Published 2026-10-21

2020: Brexit transition and cross-border planning

Retrospective: The United Kingdom left the EU in January 2020, followed by a transition period ending in December. Ireland remained an EU member state, while trade and mobility arrangements involving the UK changed. Practical effects depend on nationality, goods and services.

Retrospective / Analysis · Published 2026-10-21

2021: EU–UK trading arrangements enter a new phase

Retrospective: The Trade and Cooperation Agreement provisionally applied from January 2021. Businesses with UK-linked operations had to review customs, regulatory and service arrangements. Ireland–Germany trade within the EU follows a different framework.

Retrospective / Analysis · Published 2026-10-21

2022: Energy pressures affect European businesses

Retrospective: Energy-market disruptions and inflation created cost pressures across European economies. Employers and relocating professionals increasingly needed to compare compensation with housing, transport and energy costs.

Retrospective / Analysis · Published 2026-10-21

2023: EU digital policy continues to develop

Retrospective: European digital regulation expanded with phased obligations under the Digital Services Act and related legislation. Requirements differ by service type and business role; this is not a statement that every consultancy is covered.

Retrospective / Analysis · Published 2026-10-21

2024: EU AI Act adopted

Retrospective: The EU adopted its AI Act in 2024, with obligations phased in over time. Organizations using AI in recruitment should assess their specific systems, roles and applicable dates before making compliance claims.

Retrospective / Analysis · Published 2026-10-21

2025: European labour-market data remains essential

Retrospective analysis: Cross-border hiring decisions should be grounded in current vacancy, wage and employment data rather than unsupported claims of talent shortages. National and EU statistical series can provide useful context.
