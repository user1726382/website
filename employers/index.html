Employer questions

What roles do you focus on?

Our initial focus is German-speaking commercial and customer-facing roles, including sales, account management, customer success and support. We can discuss adjacent specialist needs.

Are positions based in Ireland?

Ireland-based German-speaking roles are our initial focus. Tell us the location, working arrangement and language requirements for your brief.

How do you assess language requirements?

We first clarify the German and English proficiency needed for the job. Any assessment approach is agreed for the specific assignment.

Can you help define a role?

We can discuss responsibilities, experience, salary expectations, working model and candidate priorities.

What does recruitment involve?

We begin with a discovery conversation and agree the search scope. Subsequent sourcing, assessment and interview support depend on the engagement.

What are your fees?

Scope, commercial terms and fees are agreed before an engagement. No standard rate or guarantee is implied.

Can you also advise on DACH expansion?

Market entry, commercial positioning and cross-border marketing can be discussed as separate consulting work.
